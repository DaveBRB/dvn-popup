# Credits

Concept project by David Berrebi — not affiliated with Dries Van Noten.

- **Scene**: modelled in Blender; gravel, tree and other textures in the .blend are from Poly Haven (CC0).
- **Avenue trees** (`assets/trees.glb`): generated with [EZ-Tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree)
  1.1.0 and its bark and leaf textures (leaves recoloured for autumn). Licence below.
- **Traffic sound**: synthesised in the browser with the Web Audio API (`ambient.js`).
- **Birdsong** (`assets/birds.mp3`): calls cut from field recordings on [Freesound](https://freesound.org)
  released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain), with background
  noise reduced. Attribution isn't required, but thanks to the recordists:
  - Blackbird: [Single Blackbird In The Evening](https://freesound.org/people/Kinoton/sounds/393517/) by Kinoton,
    [Common Blackbird 13_seq](https://freesound.org/people/tinga/sounds/195909/) by tinga
  - Robin: [A Robin Singing At The Summer Cafe](https://freesound.org/people/WhiteNoiseSleeper/sounds/725609/) by WhiteNoiseSleeper,
    [robin song](https://freesound.org/people/Andrew.soundscape/sounds/671058/) by Andrew.soundscape
  - Great tit: [Great Tit.wav](https://freesound.org/people/straget/sounds/418102/) by straget,
    [Great Tit](https://freesound.org/people/D4XX/sounds/607242/) by D4XX
  - Blue tit: [BlueTit](https://freesound.org/people/DistinctlyAverage/sounds/872964/) by DistinctlyAverage
  - Wren: [Wren singing](https://freesound.org/people/freemaster2/sounds/849624/) by freemaster2,
    [The song of an Eurasian Wren](https://freesound.org/people/hoshisato/sounds/853671/) by hoshisato
  - Chaffinch: [Common Chaffinch (Fringilla coelebs) song](https://freesound.org/people/Sacha.Julien/sounds/725328/) by Sacha.Julien,
    [Chaffinch.wav](https://freesound.org/people/straget/sounds/423701/) by straget
  - House sparrows: [Sparrows01](https://freesound.org/people/Kimskell/sounds/628949/) and
    [Sparrows02](https://freesound.org/people/Kimskell/sounds/628951/) by Kimskell,
    [House sparrows](https://freesound.org/people/freemaster2/sounds/670176/) by freemaster2,
    [Sparrows Close Chirp Exterior](https://freesound.org/people/Profispiesser/sounds/543827/) by Profispiesser
  - Magpie: [Magpie.wav](https://freesound.org/people/straget/sounds/418110/) by straget,
    [Eurasian Magpie Calling](https://freesound.org/people/naturenotesuk/sounds/555096/) by naturenotesuk
  - Carrion crow: [Kraehenruf](https://freesound.org/people/cfrooos/sounds/181088/) by cfrooos
  - Wood pigeon: [pigeon.wav](https://freesound.org/people/squashy555/sounds/319512/) by squashy555,
    [Wood Pigeon Call](https://freesound.org/people/D4XX/sounds/607224/) by D4XX
- **Libraries** (loaded from jsDelivr): [three.js](https://threejs.org) 0.180.0 (MIT),
  [three-mesh-bvh](https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-mesh-bvh) 0.9.16 (MIT).
